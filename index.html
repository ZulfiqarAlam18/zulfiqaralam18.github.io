About Me

I'm a Software Engineer focused on Flutter. At Sapphire Consulting Service I build responsive UI and API-driven features, track down production issues and support releases. Before that I spent two years freelancing, taking client apps from the first screen to a Play Store release.

Most of my work pairs a Flutter front end with a Node.js/Express or FastAPI backend on AWS (EC2, S3, Cognito). I've led small teams of Flutter and backend developers, set up CI/CD with GitHub Actions, and integrated payments, push notifications and ML inference into shipped apps.
